### **How to Participate in a Study on OpenRadBench (ORB)**

**1. Access the Datasets Tab**

* Click the folder icon on the left-hand navigation menu to open the **datasets** tab.

**2. Select a Study and Enter Your Token**

* In the sidebar, you will see a list of active studies. Click on the study you wish to participate in (e.g., "tmp").
* Select or enter your unique access token in the input field just below the study name.
* Click the **Run** (play) button to load the cases assigned to you.

**3. Open a Case and View the Modality**

* Your assigned cases (e.g., `pat02`, `pat01`) will now appear in the sidebar.
* Expand a case folder, then expand the study folder within it.
* Click on a specific modality (e.g., `BrtF5-SSA-0301-C`) to load the medical scan into the main viewer.

**4. Review the Labels**

* In the sidebar, expand the **Labels** section for that specific case and select the label file.
* The scan will populate with the annotator's highlighted regions. You can view the specific categories (e.g., Core, Edema, Enhancing) from the top toolbar.
* **Optional:** If you spot an anomaly that does not fit the predefined categories, you can click **+ Add label** in the top dropdown menu to flag it for the study administrators.

**5. Submit Quality Feedback**

* In the left sidebar, navigate to the **Status/Feedback** section.
* Under **Label Status**, open the survey.
* Rate the quality of the provided label on the 1 to 5 scale (e.g., selecting '5' for "Clinic ready") and click **Submit**.

**6. Edit the Annotations (If Necessary)**

* Change the **Label Status** dropdown to **In Progress**.
* Select the **Pen** tool from the top toolbar.
* Adjust your viewing angle using the layout options on the top right (e.g., selecting **Axial**).
* Select the specific label category you want to apply (e.g., "Core") from the top menu, and draw directly on the scan to fix or fill in missing areas.
* Click the **Save** icon (floppy disk) at the very top of the screen once you are satisfied with your edits.

**7. Mark the Case as Done**

* Once your work is saved, go back to the **Label Status** dropdown and select **Done**.
* A pop-up will appear asking you to confirm completion. **Note:** Once marked as done, this action cannot be undone unless an administrator reverses it for you.
* Click **Confirm** to lock the case and notify the reviewers that your analysis is complete.

**8. Move to the Next Case**

* With the current case finished, you can now select the next patient case (e.g., `pat01`) from the sidebar and repeat the process.
